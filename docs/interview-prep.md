# FDE interview prep

**Areas**
- Coding: Python, API integration, data wrangling
- Systems: design an AI app (RAG or agent) with auth, observability, cost, failure modes
- Debugging: given logs and configs, find the fault
- Customer scenarios: scoping, pushback, trade-offs, delivering bad news
- Behavioral: ownership, ambiguity, conflict, speed vs quality

**Practice prompts**
1. Design a support assistant over 10k internal documents. How do you evaluate it, secure it, and bound cost?
2. A customer's RAG answers are confidently wrong. Walk through your investigation.
3. Design an ops agent that can restart services. Where are the approval gates and what is the blast radius?
4. A POC works in a demo but fails on real data. What do you do in the first 48 hours?
5. Explain prompt vs RAG vs fine-tuning to a non-technical VP.

Record yourself answering; review for structure, trade-offs and clarity.
